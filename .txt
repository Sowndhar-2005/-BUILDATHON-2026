Education Management Portal

- Manage students, teachers, courses, classes, assignments, attendance, examinations, and academic records.
- Provide Home, Courses, Contact, and Course Details with search, filtering, schedules, and enrollment.
- Allow teachers to manage courses, classes, assignments, examinations, and academic activities.
- Provide students with a simple platform to manage their academic activities.

Student & Teacher Experience

- Students can access courses, submit assignments, check attendance, view results, and track progress.
- Provide personalized AI recommendations to help students improve their performance.
- Teachers can record attendance, evaluate assignments, conduct examinations, enter marks, and monitor students.
- Dashboards provide profiles, courses, assignments, grades, progress, and academic insights.

AI Powered Academic Intelligence

- Analyze attendance, assignment scores, examination marks, and academic performance.
- Identify weak subjects, academic risks, and important performance trends.
- Generate personalized recommendations for student improvement.
- Provide AI insights to teachers and administrators for better decisions.

Administration & Reports

- Administrators can manage students, teachers, courses, classes, examinations, and academic records.
- Provide performance analytics, comparative reports, activity monitoring, and AI insights.
- Generate reports covering progress, weak areas, risk analysis, and recommendations.
- Support students, teachers, and administrators with meaningful academic insights.
